Bottleneck Analysis

Identify production constraints using utilization metrics

Detection Method (Research Paper Approach)

Consistent with the research paper, the bottleneck is identified as the machine with the highest Busy-State Utilization Percentage:

Utilization (%) = (Busy Time / Total Available Time) × 100

The paper identifies Drilling as the primary bottleneck with ~85.5% busy-state percentage. Our simulation calculates this dynamically — if any other machine becomes the highest-utilization station, it will be shown as the bottleneck instead.
